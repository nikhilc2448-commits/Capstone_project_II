# Business Insights Report

## Key Insights
1. South leads regional sales at ₹77,071,377.50, representing 53.5% of total sales.
2. Electronics is the top product category at ₹87,694,175.00 in sales.
3. South contributes the highest regional profit at ₹5,558,493.09.
4. Average order value is ₹28,882.84, while overall profit margin is 8.5%.
5. Occasional / Lower-Value Customers is the largest customer segment with 374 customers and a repeat-customer rate of 98.8% across customers with orders.

## Recommendations
1. Protect South's sales position with retention and cross-sell activity aimed at its highest-value customers.
2. Expand Electronics through bundles and complementary products to improve the ₹28,882.84 average order value.
3. Use behavior-based customer campaigns and monitor the Return/Cancel Rate rate while keeping the Repeat Customer Rate rate strong.

## KPI Assessment

**Best-performing KPI:** Repeat Customer Rate. Repeat Customer Rate is the strongest customer-health KPI because 98.83% of customers with orders are repeat customers, indicating strong repeat purchasing behavior.

**Worst-performing KPI:** Return/Cancel Rate. Return/Cancel Rate is the clearest improvement opportunity because 13.92% of orders are cancelled or returned, which can create avoidable service and profitability pressure.
